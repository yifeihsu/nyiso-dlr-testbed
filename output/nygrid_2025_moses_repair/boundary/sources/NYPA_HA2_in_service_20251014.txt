Dear Secretary Phillips:

Certificate Condition 42 of the New York State Public Service Commission's Order Granting Certificate of
Environmental Compatibility and Public Need, issued in the above-referenced proceeding, approving the Smart
Path Connect Project, requires that" Within ten (10) days after each line of the Project is in service, the Certificate
Holder shall notify the Secretary in writing of that fact." This letter provides the required notification that on
October 13, 2025, the New York Power Authority energized the Haverstock-Adirondack 2 transmission line
("HA2").

The energization of the HA2 is a major milestone for the Smart Path Connect Project, and a vital step to providing
a crucial pathway for renewable energy that will allow New York State to achieve its goal of a clean energy
future.
