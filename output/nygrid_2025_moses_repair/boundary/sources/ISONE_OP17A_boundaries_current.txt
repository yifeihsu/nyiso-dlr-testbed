I. Background

   The ISO New England Reliability Coordinator Area/Balancing Authority Area (RCA/BAA)
   is currently divided into 11 areas based on voltage/reactive performance, the number and
   strength of interconnections between voltage levels, and physical characteristics such as
   length of transmission lines, overhead or underground construction, etc. These 11 area
   definitions contained in the following tables may be updated from time-to-time, as system
   changes dictate.
