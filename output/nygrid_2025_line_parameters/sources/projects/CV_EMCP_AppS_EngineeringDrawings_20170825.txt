                 Vehicle Turning Radius and Access Road Widening in Corners

Access Roads – General condition and site specific conditions were evaluated
   1. General cases of a proposed 15’ wide spur road intersecting an existing 15’ wide access
      road to determine the inside curve and widening at the turn for the access road.
   2. Turning at specific sites: CV-01, CV-02, CV-20, CV-25, CV-32 & CV-37


Investigated 3 Vehicle Types
   1. ASHTO SU-40 – Single Unit truck 40 ft long and 8.5 ft wide representing a large line
      truck
   2. A Wb-67 Double steering tractor trailer representing a 73 ft long tractor trailer, the
      trailer is 43.5 ft long for carrying up to 55 ft long pole sections (a 10’ overhang). The
      trailer has rear end steering. For this analysis, we assumed that the rear wheel steering
      is linked 1:1 with the front-end steering. (The photo of the trailer did not reveal a
      seating location for a second driver.)
   3. Tracked bucket trucks and tracked cranes. Because they are tracked and one track can
      turn independently of the other it is assumed that they can easily turn tight corners with
      minimal radius widening. No modeling was conducted.

Results
General configuration for Wb-67 double steering tractor trailer or any other construction
equipment – Widen road in the vicinity of the intersection by 8 feet and use a 50ft inside curve
radius for 90° corners or intersections.
General configuration for trucks or construction vehicles up to 40 feet long - Widen road in the
vicinity of the intersection by 5 feet and use a 35ft inside curve radius for 90° corners or
intersections.
