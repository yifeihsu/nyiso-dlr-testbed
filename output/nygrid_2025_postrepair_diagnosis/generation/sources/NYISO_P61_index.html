NYISO Generation Economic Dispatch Analysis Chart
Allows users to view and analyze a daily chart of NYISO's actual physical real-time dispatch vs. ideal economic dispatch. This report will provide a chart of on-dispatch generators': (1) total actual dispatch above ideal economic dispatch, (2) total actual dispatch below ideal economic dispatch, and (3) total actual dispatch below ideal economic dispatch with an adjustment to eliminate block loaded GTs.
