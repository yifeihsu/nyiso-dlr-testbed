# Infrastructure status at the 2025 cutoff

Audit date: 2026-09-08. Physical cutoff: 2025-12-31. This is a separate evidence audit of the selected infrastructure in `apply_compact_2025_infrastructure.m`; it does not change the frozen 66-bus electrical case, parameters, results, thermal assumptions or generation-reconstruction protocol. Later publications are used retrospectively to establish commissioning milestones, not presented as information available in 2025. No parameter recommendation is inferred from interface-fit errors.

**The important correction is that Smart Path Connect was partly energized in 2025.** Full-project completion in June 2026 does not justify saying that every component was unavailable in 2025. The frozen case omits those partial upgrades and should be described as a selected historical-template approximation. The helper's `230kV_through_cutoff` label and blanket `excluded_after_2025_cutoff` status overstate the evidence. Keeping the case frozen is a modeling decision; it is separate from documented commissioning.

The accompanying [status register](infrastructure_cutoff_status_audit.csv) contains 28 project/component records. The [source manifest](infrastructure_status_source_manifest.csv) records 32 primary sources, public URLs, retrieval date, cached byte hashes where available, and explicit download failures. Six owner pages/PDFs were reviewed through public web text but rejected direct byte downloads; their SHA-256 fields are blank, not fabricated. Raw source files remain under `tmp/infrastructure_status_audit/`, apart from the previously cached NYISO Gold Book. The DPS document list contained 874 filings; the audit selected 16 owner in-service notices, rather than interpreting forecast dates as completed work.

## Smart Path and Smart Path Connect

The original Smart Path rebuild's final segment entered service in May 2023. NYPA's June 20, 2023 completion announcement distinguishes 345-kV design capability from initial operation at 230 kV. This supports the original rebuild date, but not a claim that every circuit remained at 230 kV throughout 2025. Smart Path is the two-circuit Moses–Adirondack core; the model's two additional Adirondack–Porter templates are supporting historical equivalents, not two more Smart Path core circuits. [NYPA completion announcement](https://www.nypa.gov/News/Press-Releases/2023/20230620-78-mile), [2023 green-bond report](https://nypa.gov/-/media/nypa/documents/document-library/financials/2023-nypa-green-bond-report.pdf).

These dates come from the owners' notices in DPS Case 21-T-0340: [HA2](https://documents.dps.ny.gov/public/Common/ViewDoc.aspx?DocRefId=%7B7068E499-0000-C447-80CF-7A033EE81A59%7D), [Line 11](https://documents.dps.ny.gov/public/Common/ViewDoc.aspx?DocRefId=%7B804FE499-0000-C12E-8CA7-F547F681A4FA%7D), [Line 13](https://documents.dps.ny.gov/public/Common/ViewDoc.aspx?DocRefId=%7B50BC9D9A-0000-CA11-81D7-A2EA72DF9164%7D), [HA1](https://documents.dps.ny.gov/public/Common/ViewDoc.aspx?DocRefId=%7B101D3F9D-0000-CA6E-AF99-F8341FEECB4B%7D), [Line 12](https://documents.dps.ny.gov/public/Common/ViewDoc.aspx?DocRefId=%7B30EE449D-0000-C554-9B1F-EEA1FBA5E0FB%7D). The HA2 notice itself omits voltage; its 345-kV identity is corroborated by the [November 7, 2025 NYISO TTC report](https://mis.nyiso.com/public/pdf/ttcf/20251107ttcf.pdf) and the [2025 Gold Book, Table VII](https://www.nyiso.com/documents/20142/2226333/2025-Gold-Book-Public.pdf/088438e1-02f1-5316-211b-dbca17c01b4b). Gold Book proposed dates are not used as commissioning proof.

Additional owner notices document WRY1 on September 23; HW2 on October 2; MH2 on October 8; W1 on October 22; WPN1 on October 24; MH3 on October 26; and WPN2 on November 14, 2025. Their notices and hashes are individually registered. The [NYISO October operations report](https://www.nysrc.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/7.3.2-Operations_Report_202510_v1-long-form-Attachment-7.3.2.pdf) identifies HW2 as 345 kV; MH2/MH3, WRY1, WPN1 and W1 as 230 kV; and Haverstock AT2/AT3 and WillisAnnex TR2 as 345/230-kV facilities brought into service during the October buildout. This audit leaves voltage blank for notices whose voltage was not independently verified.

An energization milestone does not prove continuous operational availability. For example, the November 7 TTC report still lists a HA2 outage through November 15. Its published transfer capabilities are not actual line-flow measurements. The October operator summary groups WRY1 with October additions although the owner's notice gives September 23 energization; both are retained rather than silently changing the owner's date. NYPA's [whole-project completion announcement](https://www.nypa.gov/News/Press-Releases/2026/20260622-smarthpath) is recorded at month precision because its displayed June 17 publication date differs from the June 22 URL/news-index date.

## Proposed small partial-SPC alternative — not implemented

The evidence supports a separately named year-end-2025 alternative while preserving the 66-bus reference. A compact design can remain far below 200 buses:

1. Anchor the northern source at existing **bus 48, MOSES E, 230 kV** and the southern receiving end at **bus 43, EDIC, 345 kV**. Add distinct Haverstock 230/345-kV sections, Adirondack 345 kV and Austin Road 345 kV. Connect Moses–Haverstock MH2/MH3, registered Haverstock transformers, **one HA2 circuit**, Line 13 and Line 11. Do not activate HA1 or Adirondack–Marcy Line 12 as 2025 equipment. The circuit name/voltage/date register supplies topology evidence, not impedances.
2. Add WillisAnnex 345/230-kV sections and HW2 for the northern extension. Existing **bus 49, PLATTSBURGH, 115 kV** is a regional endpoint, not Willis, Ryan or Patnode. A minimal version can retain one explicitly assumed WillisAnnex–Plattsburgh regional equivalent for the 230-kV local network; its underlying W1/WRY1/WPN1/WPN2 coverage must be registered rather than labeling the equivalent as any one physical circuit. Retire/replace overlapping **NPCC_S7:BRANCH_ROW:220**, the existing 49–48 regional path, once. Explicit Ryan/Patnode/Willis stations are an optional few-bus extension requiring a verified connection map and downstream accounting. Do not attach a 230-kV circuit directly to the 115-kV Plattsburgh bus.
3. The four present `NY2025:SMART_PATH:1..4` records need declared replacement dispositions. Records 1/2 use PERFORM keys `1220_1230_1/2` on 1220–48; records 3/4 use `1220_1232_1/2` on 1220–44. Their source circuit numbering has **not** been proven to correspond individually to HA1/HA2 or Lines 11/12/13. One defensible compact approximation is whole-functional retirement of all four and replacement by the documented single 345-kV route. This is a proposed aggregation choice, not a proved four-device retirement crosswalk. It deliberately omits any unverified transitional 230-kV parallel route. Keep the already-retired parent row47 off. Retire the resulting unused, added zero-injection Adirondack230 bus1220, or give any retained connection separate evidence; do not leave an isolated bus.
4. This version needs six new station sections and removal of the unused added bus1220: approximately **71 buses**, before optional local detail. Preserve all original 46 NPCC NY buses, their demand/generation accounting and fixed external-boundary inputs. The existing Edic–Porter and Porter115 supply functions require an explicit retained-versus-replaced ledger; commissioning notices do not establish their retirement dates. Validate islanding, duplicate functional paths, voltage levels and fresh bounded AC/PF independently before qualifying the alternative.

New line R/X/B, transformer impedances/taps, shunts and circuit ratings remain unresolved. Any historical per-length parameter template, transformer proxy or conductor selection must be preregistered as an assumption with a bounded sensitivity, independently of interface residuals. A 230-kV per-unit template cannot simply be relabeled 345 kV without an explicit physical/base conversion. Thermal eligibility must be reassessed per replacement circuit; historical electrical keys alone do not prove an installed conductor or overhead construction. This audit proposes no optimized parameter values and makes no numerical feasibility or improved-fit claim for the alternative.
